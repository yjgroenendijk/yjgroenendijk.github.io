    Appointments, education & funding

    Academic and professional experience

    • Postdoctoral Researcher, Eindhoven University of Technology · Apr 2026–present. Based in Built Environment, working across environmental psychology, ageing and care, organisational research, architecture and technology. I develop frameworks, indicators and instruments on accessibility, agency, participation, ageing in place and person–environment fit. My work includes Living Lab design, field observations, stakeholder-engaged research on technology use and non-use in residential care and community settings, and research protocols and guidance for collaborative work with care organisations and community partners.
    • Lecturer, Erasmus University Rotterdam · Sep 2023–Nov 2025. I designed and delivered student-centred activities for BA, pre-master’s and MA students, with guidance on research design, qualitative analysis, academic writing and methodological reflection. I supervised more than 20 internships, served as second reader for BA and MA theses and received the 2025 PCMC Teaching Award. My CV records average student evaluations of 4.8/5.0. I completed RISBO university didactics training and am willing to obtain the University Teaching Qualification (BKO).
    • GGZ Nurse Trainee, Antes · Sep–Nov 2025. I worked alongside multidisciplinary mental-health and addiction-care professionals supporting people facing intersecting mental health, addiction and housing challenges. The role gave me first-hand experience of care practices and barriers to service access.
    • Doctoral Researcher, Erasmus University Rotterdam · Sep 2019–Sep 2025. I designed qualitative research combining in-depth interviews, participant observation and digital ethnography with narrative, thematic and critical framing analysis. I developed conceptual and analytical frameworks grounded in fieldwork and digital materials to study older migrants’ agency, digital practices and belonging. My supervisors were Prof. Susanne Janssen and Dr Tonny Krijnen.
    • Visiting & Affiliated Researcher, University of Helsinki · Sep 2017–May 2019. I compared educational policy, social justice and institutional practice in China and Finland, examining how concepts and evidence travel across contexts. I published peer-reviewed research, co-edited special issues and co-authored a monograph on teachers’ continuing professional development.
    • Earlier experience · Internship at Tencent in 2017. Additional study in Social Pedagogy at Hokkaido University, Japan.
