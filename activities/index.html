    Community-engaged research and societal impact

    • Memory as Care (2026–2027): co-applicant on a funded participatory oral-history project with university and Rotterdam community partners. The project is developing memory cafés and oral-history interviews; a public exhibition is planned.
    • Ageing-friendly neighbourhoods: research with ELLI and MOVUS on accessibility, agency and participation, including approaches for engaging residents and care partners in Living Lab settings.
    • Knowledge exchange: invited academic talks, early-career researcher workshops and public-facing commentary on older adults’ digital lives.
