    Publications

    Selected publications

    These studies illustrate my work across migration, social belonging, organisational behaviour, digital life and social justice. The full publication record is listed below.

    A tale of two cities: Digital place-making and elderly Houniao migration in China · Journal of Ethnic and Migration Studies, 2023. Older migrants’ mobility, place-making and belonging.

    Gain or Pain: The Double-Edged Sword Effect of Artificial Intelligence Adoption on Employee Service Performance · Journal of Organizational Behavior, 2025. A collaborative study of technology adoption and workplace behaviour.

    “Vlogging my lives of two homes” · Mobile Media & Communication, 2026. Older seasonal migrants’ digital practices, agency and sense of home.

    Comparing and contrasting how social justice is “done” in education? · Education and Society, 2018. Comparative reflections on social justice and institutional practice in China and Finland.

    Care recipients, active agers, or lifestyle seekers? · Media Asia, 2026. Social representations of older seasonal migrants over time.

    Selected theoretical contributions

    Warm Experts 3.0 examines older adults' peer expertise and digital care (IJCS); the co-authored Houniao Frame explains how institutional constraints shape media representations of older migrants (Media Asia). My research on digital place-making connects mobility, everyday practices and belonging (JEMS; MMC). Theoretical approach.
